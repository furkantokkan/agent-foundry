# Marketing CRM Playbook

Use this when the task involves finding who to follow, who to pitch, or how to
build a launch CRM for an indie Steam page.

## CRM Fields

Track at least:

- Name
- Type: journalist, creator, platform, studio, festival, community, curator
- Outlet or channel
- Contact URL
- Social URL
- Email or form if public
- Relevant competitor/source URL
- Why they fit this game
- Audience fit: genre, tone, platform, mechanic, audience size
- Priority: A, B, C
- Suggested angle
- Status: research, followed, warm, pitched, replied, declined, covered
- Last touch date
- Next action
- UTM source/medium/campaign to use

Do not add random large accounts without a relevance reason.

## Competitor Research Loop

1. List comparable games on Steam by genre, camera, mechanics, mood, and
   audience fantasy.
2. Open each competitor Steam page and capture:
   - developer/publisher name
   - website and social links
   - tags
   - capsule/trailer/screenshot patterns
   - review language from players
   - press quotes or outlet mentions
3. Search the studio name and game title across web, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit,
   X/Twitter, and press sites.
4. Add journalists, creators, curators, festivals, and communities that covered
   those competitor games.
5. Write the relevance reason in the CRM before outreach.

## Press Research

Start with sites that regularly cover games, then search for indie-specific
articles and competitor coverage:

- IGN and IGN Worldwide
- GameSpot
- PC Gamer
- Rock Paper Shotgun
- Polygon
- Kotaku
- Game Informer

Search pattern:

```text
site:example.com "indie game" "your genre"
site:example.com "competitor game title"
"competitor game title" interview
"competitor game title" review
"competitor game title" preview
```

Add the article author, not just the outlet. Pitch the author only if their beat
matches the game.

## Creators and Streamers

Seed list to research:

- Best Indie Games
- Splattercat
- Wanderbot
- Northernlion

Expand from:

- YouTube videos covering competitor games.
- Steam Curators on competitor pages.
- TikTok posts with similar gameplay hooks.
- Twitch categories and VODs for comparable games.

Creator fit matters more than raw size. Add expected angle: first look, demo
preview, challenge run, cozy showcase, strategy breakdown, dev interview, or
festival roundup.

## Outreach Guardrails

- Build a relationship before asking when possible: follow, engage, and track
  useful context.
- Personalize the first two sentences around their actual coverage.
- Lead with a gameplay GIF/trailer, Steam link, one-sentence hook, release/demo
  date, platform, and press kit.
- Do not mass-send identical pitches.
- Do not claim virality, awards, platform support, or content that does not
  exist.

## Useful Outreach Angles

- New Steam Coming Soon page and announcement trailer.
- Playable demo available.
- Next Fest participation.
- A specific mechanic that is easy to show in a 5-15 second clip.
- A developer story only if it strengthens the game pitch.
- Major update, cross-promotion, or festival selection.

## Cross-Promotion

Look for developers with similar audience but non-competing release timing.
Potential formats:

- Steam announcement swap.
- Newsletter mention.
- Demo bundle or event collection.
- Social repost with a specific gameplay angle.

Use tracked links and log results in the CRM.

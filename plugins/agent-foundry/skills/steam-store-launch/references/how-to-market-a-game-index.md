## Action Heuristics To Apply

- Prefer visible gameplay clips over pure key art or vague announcements.
- Treat competitor research as source discovery: comparable games reveal press,
  creators, curators, tags, screenshots, capsule patterns, and audience language.
- Use demo and Next Fest strategy only after confirming current Steamworks
  eligibility, demo review deadlines, and event dates.
- Measure campaigns with Steam UTM links and compare visits, wishlist additions,
  deletions, conversion rate, and source quality.
- Do not promise a benchmark number without checking the source article, date,
  genre, and comparable game fit.
- Do not copy article wording into outbound marketing. Extract the strategic
  lesson and write original game-specific copy.

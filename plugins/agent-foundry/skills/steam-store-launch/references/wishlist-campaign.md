# Wishlist Campaign Reference

Use this when planning the announce, social, Reddit, creator, festival, and
cross-promotion campaign around a Steam page.

## Principle

Strong indie posts usually do not rely on memes alone. They show the game
clearly: readable gameplay, a striking interaction, an immediately understood
fantasy, and a direct Steam wishlist call to action.

## Campaign Phases

### Before Page Live

- Define one-sentence hook.
- Pick 5-10 comparable Steam games and audit their pages.
- Prepare capsules, screenshots, gameplay trailer, short description, feature
  bullets, tags, and press kit.
- Build CRM from competitor coverage and creator fit.
- Prepare UTM naming before sharing links.
- Draft announcement posts for X/Twitter, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube Shorts,
  Discord, mailing list, and Steam announcement.

### Page Live Week

- Publish the Coming Soon page after approval.
- Announce with the strongest gameplay clip, not a logo-only post.
- Pin the Steam link.
- Send warm pitches first, then creator/journalist outreach.
- Post to communities only where self-promotion rules allow it.
- Track traffic and wishlist conversion by UTM.
- Save replies, objections, and player wording for page copy improvements.

### After Announcement

- Turn the strongest comments/questions into follow-up posts.
- Post a short series around mechanics, world, challenge, progression, or player
  fantasy.
- Pitch creators with a demo key when available.
- Apply to relevant festivals and showcases.
- Seek cross-promotion with similar indie teams.
- Review Steam traffic, UTM, wishlist additions, deletions, and regional data.

## Announcement Post Shapes

Use the game's real content:

- "I am making a game where [clear action/fantasy]. Steam page is live."
- "You can now wishlist [Game] on Steam. [One-sentence hook]."
- "We just opened our Steam page. Here is [most interesting mechanic] in action."
- "Our demo is live for [event/festival]. Try it and wishlist on Steam."

Avoid:

- Empty "big news soon" posts.
- Countdown posts with no new gameplay.
- Pure key art without gameplay.
- Generic "please wishlist" posts without a reason.

## Reddit Notes

Before posting:

- Read each subreddit developer/self-promotion rules.
- For PC gaming, read the developer rules page:
  https://www.reddit.com/r/pcgaming/wiki/developers
- Lead with a concrete playable or visual story.
- Participate in comments quickly and honestly.
- Do not repost the same title everywhere.

## Festivals and Showcases

Maintain a living list. Always verify current dates and requirements:

- Steam Next Fest
- Steam themed fests
- Guerrilla Collective
- Wholesome Games / Wholesome Snack
- Platform indie showcases
- Genre-specific showcases
- Local or regional game festivals

For each event, track:

- eligibility
- registration deadline
- assets required
- demo requirement
- trailer requirement
- press preview date
- public event date
- submission URL
- owner
- status

## Tracking Links

Prefer Steam UTM Analytics for Steam page links:

```text
utm_source=x
utm_medium=social
utm_campaign=steam_page_announce
utm_content=gameplay_clip_01
```

Use short links only when the platform or post format needs them, and keep the
underlying UTM clean.

## Metrics To Review

- Steam visits
- trusted/tracked visits
- wishlist additions
- wishlist deletions
- conversion by UTM source
- trailer retention when available
- top countries/regions
- creator/video referral spikes
- festival/demo traffic
- comments/questions that reveal confusion

Use the metrics to revise copy, tags, trailer order, screenshots, and outreach
targets.
